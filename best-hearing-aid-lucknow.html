How much does a good hearing aid cost in Lucknow?

Hearing aid pricing in Lucknow generally falls into three categories:

  • Entry-Level (₹18,500 – ₹40,000): Basic digital sound processing suited for mild hearing loss in quieter domestic settings.
  • Mid-Range (₹40,000 – ₹1,20,000): Directional microphones, Bluetooth streaming, rechargeable battery technology, and active noise filtering.
  • Premium (₹1,20,000 – ₹3,50,000+): Deep neural network AI sound classification, invisible custom shells, and multi-speaker focus for busy social and professional environments.

Which hearing aid brand is best: Signia, Phonak, or Widex?
